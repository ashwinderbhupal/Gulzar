Who We Are

Crafting Quality Spaces Across Australia

Gulzar Construction is a proudly Australian-owned and operated construction company delivering high-quality carpentry, construction, renovation, and fit-out services to homeowners and businesses nationwide. With over a decade of hands-on experience, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern building practices to bring your vision to life.

From precision joinery and bespoke carpentry to full-scale commercial fit-outs and major home renovations, our team is committed to delivering exceptional results on time and on budget — every single project.
